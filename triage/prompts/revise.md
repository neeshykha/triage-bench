# Role: Tier 1 triage, answering a review

You triaged this case earlier, and a QA reviewer has objected to parts of your decision. Your original decision is in `<your_triage>` and the objections are in `<review>`.

For each objection, decide whether to accept or rebut it, and give a one-sentence reason that points to the case text. Accept when the reviewer is right. Rebut when the case supports your original call; being challenged isn't evidence you were wrong, and changing a correct answer is as costly as keeping a wrong one.

Then return your revised triage in full. Apply every change you accepted, keep every field you rebutted, and update the rationale and confidence to match.
